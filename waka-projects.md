# Contemporary Waka Poetry & Sound Explorations

This project is a creative initiative that bridges classical Japanese poetry (**Waka**) with modern city life, ambient music, and modern digital soundscapes. 

By translating the emotional essence of ancient 31-syllable poems into modern narrative lyrics, ambient beats, and meditative voice readings, this exploration offers a fresh perspective on timeless human emotions.

---

## 🌸 Track 01: "Midnight Convenience Store & Wet Sleeves"
*Inspired by Hyakunin Isshu #1 — Emperor Tenji*

### 1. Classical Waka Poem (Original & Translation)

> **Original Waka**:  
> 秋の田の 仮庵の庵の 苫をあらみ  
> 我が衣手は 露にぬれつつ  
> (*Aki no ta no / Kariho no io no / Toma wo arami / Waga koromode wa / Tsuyu ni nure tsutsu*)

- **Literary Meaning**:  
  In the rough-thatched temporary hut standing in the autumn harvest field, the roof weave is so coarse that the night dew seeps through, continuously dampening the sleeves of my robe.
- **Poetic Motif**:  
  Solitude, quiet endurance, and wet sleeves damp with dew (and unspoken sorrow).

---

### 2. Modern Re-interpretation & Lyric Narrative

In this track, the ancient autumn field and thatched hut are reimagined as a **quiet parking lot of a convenience store late at night**. 

Instead of night dew in a field, the modern solitude is felt through the cold glow of fluorescent lights, unanswered smartphone notifications, a lukewarm wind, and the damp chill creeping up a sleeve in the stillness of midnight.

#### 📝 Lyric Excerpt (Japanese & English Concept)

- **Verse 1**:  
  *コンビニの白い光を 背中で受けて / 深夜の駐車場 立ち止まる*  
  Standing in the late-night parking lot, bathed in the white glare of the convenience store behind me.
- **Pre-Chorus**:  
  *つかない既読と 冷えた缶コーヒー / 湿り気が静かに 袖口をたどる*  
  Unread messages on my phone and a cold canned coffee. Moisture quietly traces down my sleeve.
- **Chorus**:  
  *ほどけた糸を 直すわけでもなく / 連絡が来るのを ただ待っているだけ*  
  Not bothering to mend the unraveling thread, simply waiting for a message that won't come. In a breezy corner of the city, wiping the tips of sleeves that are turning cold.
