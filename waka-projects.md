# Contemporary Waka Poetry & Sound Explorations

This project is a creative initiative that bridges classical Japanese poetry (**Waka**) with modern city life, ambient music, and contemporary digital soundscapes. 

By translating the emotional essence of ancient 31-syllable poems into modern narrative lyrics and ambient sound, this ongoing collection (spanning 100+ pieces) reinterprets timeless human solitude, love, and quiet resonance for the present day.
